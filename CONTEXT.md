# Dog-matching inputs

Vocabulary for comparing a dog's starting condition with historical PRO reports.

## Language

**Starting symptom:** A symptom reported as part of the dog's presenting condition or existing problems. It does not require a known diagnosis or cause; exact onset may be unspecified.

**Reported diagnosis:** The condition named in the source, with its stated certainty. A more specific diagnosis can narrow a broader match without supplying an extra independent score.

**Diagnosis detail:** An attribute such as anatomical location, stage, grade or diagnosis date. An attribute is not automatically a disease subtype.

**Treatment reaction:** A symptom attributed to a treatment in the report. An event merely happening after treatment preserves chronology without establishing causation.

**Follow-up observation:** A later report of change, including improvement, worsening or a new symptom during treatment. It is separate from the starting symptom used in the initial comparison.

**Veterinary finding:** A reported examination, imaging or laboratory finding that is not automatically an owner-observed symptom.

**Unclear relationship:** Evidence that does not establish whether an observation belongs to the starting condition or a later treatment period. It stays outside initial symptom matching until resolved.
